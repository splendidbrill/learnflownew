# LearnFlow — Product Overview

## What Is LearnFlow?

LearnFlow is an AI-powered learning platform where students upload their textbooks (PDFs) and the AI transforms them into a fully personalised learning experience — explanations, tests, reviews, and doubt-clearing — all tailored to the student's own interests and learning pace.

> **Simple version:** Upload your Physics textbook. Tell us you love cricket. LearnFlow explains Newton's Laws using cricket ball mechanics. You never forget it.

---

## The Problem We Solve

- Textbooks are written for everyone, which means they're personalised for no one
- Coaching centers charge ₹50,000–₹1,00,000/year but have no visibility into whether students actually understand the material
- Teachers find out about misconceptions only at exam time — when it's too late
- Students don't open study apps voluntarily — engagement drops after day 3
- Parents pay a fortune and have zero insight into their child's progress

---

## How It Works

```
Student uploads textbook PDF
        ↓
AI extracts and structures all chapters and content
        ↓
Student sets their interest (cricket, music, gaming, movies)
        ↓
AI explains every concept using analogies from that interest
        ↓
Student reads, chats with the chapter, takes AI-generated tests
        ↓
AI tracks what they know, what they're confused about, and what to review
        ↓
Telegram bot nudges them at the right time with spaced repetition
```

## Core Features

### 📚 Smart Book Processing
- Upload any PDF textbook
- AI reads, parses, and structures it into chapters and paragraphs
- Handles math equations, diagrams, and complex scientific content
- Works for Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Computer Science, and more

### 🧠 Personalised Explanations
- Every concept explained using the student's chosen interest (cricket, Bollywood, gaming, etc.)
- Analogy-based learning proven to improve retention significantly
- Concept diagrams auto-generated for visual learners
- Text-to-speech for audio learners

### 💬 Chat With Your Book
- Students can ask questions about any chapter
- AI answers using the book's content + their interest analogy
- Full conversation history maintained per chapter

### 📝 AI-Generated Tests
- Tests auto-generated from book content
- Multiple question types
- AI grades answers and explains why something was wrong
- Adaptive difficulty — harder when you're doing well, easier when you're struggling

### 🔄 Spaced Repetition Reviews
- AI tracks what you've learned and schedules reviews at the optimal time
- Telegram bot sends review questions directly to your phone
- You answer in Telegram — no need to open the app
- Proven method used by top medical and law students worldwide

### 🏆 Gamification
- XP points, levels, streaks, badges
- Leaderboard to compete with friends
- Keeps students engaged long-term

### 📱 Telegram Bot
- Study reminders at scheduled times
- Spaced repetition questions delivered to Telegram
- Progress updates and notifications
- Students engage where they already are — not a new app to open

## Features Schools & Coaching Centers Would Pay For

### 🎯 Live Misconception Radar
After a test, the teacher sees:
> *"14 out of 30 students believe force causes constant velocity. This is a classic misconception. Recommend 10 minutes on this tomorrow."*

No tool does this today. Teachers currently discover misconceptions at board exams — months too late.

### 📞 Weekly Parent Reports (WhatsApp/Telegram)
Every week, parents receive:
> *"Arjun studied 3 chapters this week. Strong in kinematics, struggling with Newton's 3rd law. Score improved from 54% to 71%."*

Coaching centers charge ₹1 lakh/year and give parents nothing. This alone justifies the subscription.

### 🔥 Confusion Heatmap on the Textbook
Teachers open the dashboard and see their actual textbook pages highlighted — red where students got confused, green where they didn't. Paragraph-level insight into which parts of the book are unclear.

### 🏆 Classroom Battle Mode
Two classes compete live — questions on the screen, students answer on their phones, live leaderboard. Teachers already do this manually with whiteboards. We digitise and supercharge it.

### 🎯 Exam Prediction Engine
> *"Based on your last 3 months, you will likely score 67–72 in Physics. Your biggest risk: rotational motion. Focus these 3 topics in the next 7 days."*

Data-backed exam strategy. No coaching center in India does this today.

### 🩺 Burnout & Dropout Detection
AI quietly tracks engagement patterns. If a student who used to study 45 min/day drops to 8 min/day — the teacher gets an alert. Catch dropout before it happens.
